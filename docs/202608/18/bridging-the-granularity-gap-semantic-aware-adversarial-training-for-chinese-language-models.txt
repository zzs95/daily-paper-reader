## Abstract:

Recent advances in Pre-trained and Large Language Models (PLMs and LLMs) have enabled powerful Chinese understanding. However, the practical robustness of these models remains a bottleneck, as existing adversarial training paradigms often suffer from semantic degradation and fail to respect the unique logographic structure of Chinese. Motivated by this observation, we introduce SADAT, a robust alignment framework that exploits fine-grained semantic integrity by performing adversarial defense directly within the training pipeline. In particular, we propose a dual-granularity localization mechanism. By isolating salient semantic units at both word and character levels, this strategy guides perturbations toward the most vulnerable yet semantically critical regions. Furthermore, we introduce adaptive sparse perturbation to resolve the conflict between attack efficacy and semantic preservation. This module leverages a multi-distribution candidate pool and consistency regularization to balance high-fidelity adversarial construction with semantic integrity dynamically. Extensive experiments across ten benchmarks validate SADAT's robustness in both logographic (Chinese) and alphabetic (English) settings. Results demonstrate that it effectively resolves the conflict between defensive capability and semantic integrity, consistently outperforming state-of-the-art methods on both clean and adversarial data.
