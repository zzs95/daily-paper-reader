Title: Large vessel occlusion in a carotid terminus pure arterial malformation treated with mechanical thrombectomy and early re-occlusion

URL Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00234-026-04124-3

Markdown Content:
## Abstract

Pure arterial malformations (PAMs) are rare arterial anomalies typically considered incidental, and their role in acute large vessel occlusion is not well defined. We describe a case of acute large vessel occlusion occurring in direct anatomical association with a carotid terminus PAM, treated with mechanical thrombectomy and followed by early selective re-occlusion of the malformed segment. Multimodal imaging and clinical history suggested pre-existing hemodynamic compromise and in situ thrombosis with distal embolization. Mechanical thrombectomy across the PAM segment was technically feasible and resulted in distal reperfusion, but durable patency of the malformed tract was not maintained. This case expands the recognized ischemic spectrum of PAM and suggests that selected morphologically complex lesions may behave as hemodynamically unstable substrates rather than incidental vascular variants.

## Ethics declarations

### Ethics approval

Following a case-specific request for clarification submitted by the authors regarding this retrospective fully anonymized single-patient case report, the Comitato Etico Territoriale (CET) Lazio Area 1 confirmed that Ethics Committee review, approval, or formal acknowledgment was not required for this case.

### Consent for publication

The patient was unable to provide informed consent for publication because of persistent severe receptive aphasia. Although no formally appointed legal representative was available, a close family member, reviewed the fully anonymized manuscript and completed the official Springer Nature _Consent for Publication_ form, providing written agreement for publication of this case report. All clinical information and imaging data included in the manuscript have been fully anonymized, and no identifying information is disclosed.
