## Abstract

Radiology Report Generation (RRG) tasks are to automatically generate clinical descriptions of normal and abnormal tissues based on given medical images. Two factors limit the current mainstream methods: 1) the size constraint of the medical imaging dataset available for training and 2) The limited model capacity of traditional network architecture. Large language models (LLMs) become a potential solution due to their deep architecture, massive parameter scale, and large-scale pre-training data. However, their application in RRG tasks still faces dual challenges: firstly, there is a modal gap between medical visual features and LLMs text semantic space; Secondly, there is a significant deviation between the general semantic space of LLMs and knowledge in the medical field. In addition, factors such as differences in imaging equipment and information loss during data transmission lead to problems such as blurring and noise interference in medical images. In response to the above challenges, this paper proposes a Multi-Guided and Pixel Enhancement model (MPE) to achieve targeted optimization of the LLM in RRG tasks. We implement this process through three complementary components: 1) the pixel-level image enhancement module, which enhances the fine-grained features of images; 2) The cross-modal interaction module establishes a nonlinear mapping from visual space to language space, achieving deep alignment between radiological features and text semantics; 3) The refinement-tuning pipeline enables the targeted transfer of LLM’s general semantic space to the medical professional field. The experiments on two public datasets have demonstrated the effectiveness of our method, with MPE achieving competitive performance.
